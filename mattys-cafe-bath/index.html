The story

A corner of Bath, kept warm

Matty opened the doors in 2016 with a two-group machine, his gran's bun recipe, and a room that used to be an ironmonger's. The counter is the same oak plank it was then — just more coffee rings.

The idea hasn't changed: do a short list of things properly. Espresso from a Somerset roaster we visit every month. Bath buns baked each morning the way this city has made them for three hundred years — candied peel, crushed sugar, no shortcuts. Brunch that arrives hot and doesn't try to be clever.

Mornings here are locals and crossword pages; afternoons are walkers down from the Royal Crescent thawing their hands on a mug. Come in either. Stay as long as you like.
