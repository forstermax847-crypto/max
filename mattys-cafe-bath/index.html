The story

“I just love food”

Hello, my name is Matt and I am the proud owner of Matty’s Café. For over 20 years now, I have had the pleasure of serving my food to customers all over the city of Bath.

I believe in fair prices, generous portions, and overall, a great experience. With breakfast being one of the most important meals of the day, I know how much it means to have a nice start to the day.

Also, I am one of few cafes in Bath that delivers a full English breakfast to your doorstep. Keeping open 361 days a year, proving that I am passionate about what I do.

Inside the cafe, I also have an outdoor area with up to 120 seats for the Summer when you don’t want to be sat inside. Delivery or in-person, can’t wait to see you soon.

~ Matt
