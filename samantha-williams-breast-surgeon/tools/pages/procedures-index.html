Procedures

Miss Williams's practice is breast surgery only. The pages below describe each procedure, who it suits, what the operation and recovery involve, and the risks. For cosmetic surgery, a written plan and a cooling-off period of at least two weeks are standard before anything is booked.

Cosmetic breast surgery

Breast augmentation

Enlargement with implants, sized to the patient's frame.

Breast reduction

Reducing size and weight, with the breast reshaped and lifted.

Breast uplift

Mastopexy to restore shape and position, with or without implants.

Breast symptoms and cancer care

Breast lump assessment

One-stop assessment of lumps and other symptoms, with imaging and results on the same day.

Breast cancer surgery

Oncoplastic surgery that removes the cancer while keeping the breast wherever that is safe.

Breast reconstruction

Implant-based and own-tissue reconstruction, immediate or delayed.

Other reasons patients come to clinic

You do not need to know which procedure you need. These are the concerns patients most often bring to Miss Williams. Book a consultation and she will advise what, if anything, is needed.

  • Breast lumps and changes
  • Urgent (fast-track) cancer assessment
  • Breast pain
  • Nipple inversion or discharge
  • Breast asymmetry
  • Checks of existing breast implants
  • Lipofilling after previous surgery or radiotherapy
  • Second opinions
