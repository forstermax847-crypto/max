Legal

Raising a concern or complaint

If something about your care has not been right, we want to know — promptly, and from you directly. Every concern is taken seriously and used to improve the practice.

Miss Williams is committed to providing care of the highest standard, but if you are unhappy with any aspect of your experience — clinical, administrative or personal — you have the right to raise it, and to receive a prompt, honest and courteous response. Raising a concern will never affect the care you receive.

Step one: tell us

Many concerns can be resolved quickly and informally. Please speak to Miss Williams at your appointment, or contact her medical secretary, {{secretary}}, on {{phone_display}} or at {{email}}. If you would prefer to put your concern in writing, send it to the practice at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, Moormead Road, Wroughton, Swindon SN4 9DD, marked "Complaint — private and confidential".

It helps to include your name and contact details, the dates and nature of the care concerned, what went wrong from your point of view, and what you would like to happen as a result. If you are complaining on behalf of someone else, we will need their written consent before we can share information about their care.

What happens next

  • Your complaint will be acknowledged within 3 working days.
  • It will be investigated thoroughly, with the opportunity for you to discuss it in person or by phone if you wish.
  • You will receive a full written response within 20 working days. If the investigation needs longer, you will be told why and given a revised timescale.
  • The response will explain what was found, any action taken, and — where things went wrong — an apology and an explanation of how recurrence will be prevented.

Complaints are recorded and reviewed as part of clinical governance. Your records remain confidential, and information is shared only with those involved in investigating the complaint.

Step two: the hospital

Care provided at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital — including nursing, facilities, anaesthetic and ward care — is also covered by the hospital's own complaints process. You may raise a concern with the hospital directly, and we will help you do so. Where a complaint involves both the hospital and Miss Williams's clinical care, we will work together to give you a single, coordinated response.

To complete before publication: insert the Ridgeway Hospital's complaints contact details (patient services / complaints manager, phone and email) from the hospital's current patient information.

Step three: independent review

If you remain dissatisfied after the practice and the hospital have responded, you can ask for an independent review. For private healthcare in England this is provided by the Independent Sector Complaints Adjudication Service (ISCAS), of which the hospital's operator is a subscriber. ISCAS can be contacted at iscas.cedr.com, by email at info@iscas.org.uk, or on 020 7536 6091. ISCAS normally expects the first two stages to have been completed, and a request for adjudication to be made within six months of the final response.

Concerns about a doctor's fitness to practise

The General Medical Council (GMC) regulates all doctors in the UK. If your concern is about a doctor's professional conduct or fitness to practise, rather than about the service received, you can contact the GMC at gmc-uk.org or on 0161 923 6602. Miss Williams's GMC registration number is 6130038.

Regulation

BMI The Ridgeway Hospital is registered with and inspected by the Care Quality Commission (CQC), the independent regulator of health and social care in England. The CQC does not investigate individual complaints, but you can share your experience with it at cqc.org.uk.

Clinical negligence

If you believe you have been harmed by negligent care and wish to seek compensation, you may wish to take independent legal advice. Miss Williams is a member of the Medical Defence Union. A complaint through this procedure does not affect your legal rights.
