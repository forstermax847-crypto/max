The Surgeon

Miss Samantha Williams

Consultant Oncoplastic Breast Surgeon. Trained in both cancer surgery and cosmetic breast surgery — a combination that means the aesthetic result is never an afterthought, whatever brings you to clinic.

Training and background

Samantha Williams qualified from Imperial College School of Medicine in 2005 and completed her foundation training in London before securing a run-through training number in General Surgery in the Severn Deanery in 2007. On completing her general surgical training she was awarded a Training Interface Group (TIG) Fellowship — a competitive national fellowship that provides advanced training in cosmetic, plastic and oncoplastic breast surgery under the guidance of leading breast and plastic surgeons.

That fellowship shapes how she practises today. Oncoplastic surgery sits at the junction of cancer surgery and plastic surgery, and Miss Williams brings the same techniques — and the same attention to shape, symmetry and scars — to cosmetic patients and cancer patients alike.

Current posts

She was appointed Consultant Oncoplastic Breast Surgeon at Great Western Hospital, Swindon, in October 2019, where she treats NHS patients and serves as Clinical Governance Lead for the breast surgical department, overseeing clinical audit and quality standards. She sees private patients at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital in Wroughton, a short distance from Swindon.

Specialist interests

  • Breast conservation surgery for breast cancer
  • Therapeutic mammoplasty
  • Chest wall perforator flap reconstruction, enabling removal of large cancers without the need for mastectomy
  • DIEP (own-tissue) breast reconstruction, performed in collaboration with plastic surgeons
  • Cosmetic breast surgery — augmentation, reduction and uplift
  • Lipofilling (fat transfer) after previous surgery or radiotherapy

Teaching and recognition

Miss Williams is an Undergraduate Surgical Tutor for medical students from Bristol, King's College London and Oxford. In 2017 she was awarded Severn Deanery Trainee of the Year, recognising her commitment to education and training.

Memberships and registration

  • Royal College of Surgeons of England
  • Association of Breast Surgery
  • Medical Defence Union
  • General Medical Council — registration number 6130038

Her approach

"I am committed to holistic, patient-centred care. I support enhanced recovery after surgery and recognise the importance of both physical and emotional wellbeing in achieving the best outcomes. My patients can expect empathy, clear communication and consistent support at every stage of their treatment."

In practice, that means unhurried consultations where you are listened to before you are examined; written plans you can take away and reflect on; honesty about what surgery can and cannot do; and the same surgeon at every appointment, from the first conversation to the final follow-up. Patients' own words say it more plainly than any biography — you can read their verified reviews on the home page.
