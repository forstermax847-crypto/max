Miss Samantha Williams

Consultant Oncoplastic Breast Surgeon. Trained in both cancer surgery and cosmetic breast surgery.

Training

Samantha Williams qualified from Imperial College School of Medicine in 2005 and completed foundation training in London. In 2007 she obtained a run-through training number in general surgery in the Severn Deanery. After completing general surgical training she was awarded a Training Interface Group (TIG) Fellowship, the national fellowship in cosmetic, plastic and oncoplastic breast surgery, and trained under leading breast and plastic surgeons.

Oncoplastic surgery sits between cancer surgery and plastic surgery. Miss Williams brings the same techniques, and the same attention to shape, symmetry and scars, to cosmetic and cancer patients alike.

Current posts

She was appointed Consultant Oncoplastic Breast Surgeon at Great Western Hospital, Swindon, in October 2019, where she treats NHS patients and is clinical governance lead for the breast surgical department, responsible for clinical audit and quality standards. She sees private patients at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital in Wroughton, near Swindon.

Specialist interests

  • Breast conservation surgery for breast cancer
  • Therapeutic mammoplasty
  • Chest wall perforator flap reconstruction, which allows large cancers to be removed without a mastectomy
  • DIEP (own-tissue) breast reconstruction, in collaboration with plastic surgeons
  • Cosmetic breast surgery: augmentation, reduction and uplift
  • Lipofilling (fat transfer) after previous surgery or radiotherapy

Teaching

Miss Williams is an undergraduate surgical tutor for medical students from Bristol, King's College London and Oxford. In 2017 she was named Severn Deanery Trainee of the Year for her contribution to education and training.

Memberships and registration

  • Royal College of Surgeons of England
  • Association of Breast Surgery
  • Medical Defence Union
  • General Medical Council, registration number 6130038

How she works

"I am committed to holistic, patient-centred care. I support enhanced recovery after surgery and recognise the importance of both physical and emotional wellbeing in achieving the best outcomes. My patients can expect empathy, clear communication and consistent support at every stage of their treatment."

In practice this means a full consultation before any examination, a written plan to take away, a clear account of what surgery can and cannot do, and the same surgeon at every appointment from the first consultation to the final follow-up. Independently verified reviews from her patients are on the home page.

Appointments

Miss Williams is recognised by all major private medical insurers and also sees self-paying patients. Private consultations and surgery take place at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, Swindon.
