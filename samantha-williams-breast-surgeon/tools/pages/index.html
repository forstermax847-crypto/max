Consultant breast surgeon in Swindon

Miss Samantha Williams is a consultant oncoplastic breast surgeon. She sees private patients at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital for cosmetic breast surgery, assessment of breast lumps and other symptoms, breast cancer surgery and reconstruction. She also treats NHS patients at Great Western Hospital.

Cosmetic breast surgery

Augmentation, reduction and uplift. Each operation is planned around the patient's build and what they want to change. If a different size, or a different operation, would give a better result, Miss Williams will say so at the consultation.

Breast augmentation

Enlargement with implants, sized to the patient's frame. Includes advice on implant type and placement, and on whether an uplift is also needed.

Breast reduction

Reducing size and weight to relieve neck, shoulder and back discomfort, with the breast reshaped and lifted in the same operation.

Breast uplift

Mastopexy for breasts that have dropped after pregnancy, breastfeeding or weight loss. Can be combined with an implant or a small reduction.

If you have found a lump

Most breast lumps are not cancer, but every new lump or change should be checked. Appointments for new symptoms are prioritised. Examination, imaging and any biopsy are usually completed in a single visit, and imaging results are given the same day.

Breast lump assessment

One-stop assessment of lumps, pain, nipple changes and implant concerns. No GP referral needed for self-paying patients.

Breast cancer surgery

Breast-conserving surgery, therapeutic mammoplasty, chest wall perforator flaps and mastectomy, planned through a multidisciplinary team.

Breast reconstruction

Implant-based and own-tissue reconstruction, at the time of mastectomy or later, with symmetrising surgery to the other breast where needed.

About Miss Williams

Samantha Williams qualified from Imperial College School of Medicine in 2005 and trained in general surgery in the Severn Deanery. She then completed a Training Interface Group fellowship in cosmetic, plastic and oncoplastic breast surgery, which is the national fellowship for surgeons who go on to specialise in both cancer and cosmetic breast surgery.

She was appointed consultant at Great Western Hospital in October 2019, where she is also clinical governance lead for breast surgery. Her particular interests are breast conservation surgery, therapeutic mammoplasty and chest wall perforator flap reconstruction, techniques that allow larger cancers to be removed without a mastectomy.

In her own words: "My patients can expect empathy, clear communication and consistent support at every stage of their treatment."

How a consultation works

  1. First appointment

    A full history and examination, imaging on the same day where it is needed, and a discussion of the options, including whether surgery is the right answer at all. Allow 30 to 45 minutes. You are welcome to bring someone with you.

  2. A written plan, and time to decide

    You will be given a written summary of what was discussed, including risks, alternatives and costs. For cosmetic surgery there is a cooling-off period of at least two weeks before anything is booked, in line with General Medical Council guidance. A second consultation can be arranged if you want one.

  3. Surgery

    Operations take place at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital with a consultant anaesthetist. Miss Williams performs the surgery herself.

  4. Follow-up

    Review appointments are usually at one week, six weeks and six months. The team can be contacted between appointments if anything is worrying you.

Common questions

Do I need a GP referral?

Not if you are paying for the consultation yourself. If you are using private medical insurance, most insurers ask for a GP referral before they will authorise cover, so check with them first.

How quickly can I be seen about a lump?

Contact Tracy, Miss Williams's medical secretary. Appointments for new symptoms are prioritised, usually within a few days, with imaging and any biopsy on the same day.

How long is there between a cosmetic consultation and surgery?

At least two weeks. General Medical Council guidance on cosmetic procedures requires a cooling-off period so that you can consider the written plan, ask further questions and change your mind without pressure.

Will Miss Williams do the operation herself?

Yes. She sees you at the consultation, performs the surgery and sees you at every follow-up appointment.

If I have breast cancer, will I need a mastectomy?

Not necessarily. Techniques such as therapeutic mammoplasty and chest wall perforator flap reconstruction allow many larger cancers to be removed while keeping the breast. Whether that is appropriate depends on the size, position and type of the cancer.

Is cosmetic breast surgery covered by insurance?

Usually not. Procedures chosen for cosmetic reasons are normally self-funded. Reconstruction and medically necessary surgery are usually covered, subject to your policy and pre-authorisation.

Does Miss Williams only see private patients?

No. She is a consultant at Great Western Hospital, where she treats NHS patients, and sees private patients at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital.

Can I see Miss Williams for a second opinion?

Yes. Second opinions on a diagnosis, a treatment plan or the result of previous surgery are a regular part of her practice.
