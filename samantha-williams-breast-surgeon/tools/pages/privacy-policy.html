Privacy and cookie policy

How your personal information is used when you contact the practice or use this website, and your rights under UK data protection law.

This policy explains what information is collected through this website and in the course of arranging and providing private care, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it. It applies to the private practice. Information held about you as an NHS patient is governed by the NHS trust's own policies.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Miss Samantha Williams, Consultant Oncoplastic Breast Surgeon, practising privately at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, Moormead Road, Wroughton, Swindon SN4 9DD. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to {{email}}, or by post to the practice at the address above, marked for the attention of the medical secretary.

Information collected

When you contact the practice

If you call, email, message on WhatsApp or use the enquiry form, the details you provide are collected: typically your name, phone number, email address, the reason for your enquiry, how you would like to be contacted, and any message you include. Please do not include detailed medical information in the enquiry form.

When you become a patient

Arranging and providing care involves creating health records: your medical history, examination findings, imaging and test results, correspondence with your GP and other clinicians, consent forms, and details needed for billing and insurance. Where your care is provided at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, the hospital also holds records about you under its own privacy policy.

When you use the website

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site: pages visited, how you arrived, approximate location derived from your IP address, and device and browser type. IP addresses are anonymised. No analytics cookies are set unless you accept them.

Why the information is used, and the legal basis

  • Responding to your enquiry and arranging appointments: necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract for care.
  • Providing clinical care and keeping accurate records: necessary for the provision of health care. Health information is special category data and is processed under the provisions for health care in UK data protection law.
  • Communicating with your GP, the hospital, insurers and other clinicians involved in your care: necessary for the provision of care and, for insurers, to perform the contract with you.
  • Billing and accounting: necessary to perform the contract and to comply with legal obligations.
  • Website analytics: only with your consent.
  • Complying with legal and professional obligations, including General Medical Council requirements, clinical governance and responding to complaints.

Your information is not sold or used for marketing without your consent.

Who the information is shared with

Only as necessary for your care or to meet legal obligations: BMI The Ridgeway Hospital and its clinical staff; your GP; other clinicians involved in your care, such as radiologists, pathologists, anaesthetists and plastic surgeons; your private medical insurer, with your authority; the practice's medical secretary and secretarial and billing services; and regulators or courts where required by law. Service providers who process data on the practice's behalf, such as the enquiry-form provider and email services, are bound by contract to protect it.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment are deleted within 12 months. Clinical records are kept in line with the Records Management Code of Practice for Health and Social Care, generally for at least 8 years after your last treatment and longer in some circumstances, for example records relating to implants. Financial records are kept for 6 years as required by law.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access the information held about you; to have inaccurate information corrected; to ask for information to be erased where there is no good reason to keep it; to restrict or object to certain processing; to receive your data in a portable form; and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. To exercise any of these rights, contact the practice using the details above. You will receive a response within one month. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites you visit. This site uses two kinds.

Strictly necessary

A single setting is stored in your browser to remember whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, so that you are not asked on every page. This is not a tracking cookie and is not shared with anyone.

Analytics (optional)

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 sets cookies named _ga and _ga_*, lasting up to 2 years, to distinguish visitors and measure how the site is used. Data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy, with IP anonymisation enabled. You can decline analytics cookies when you first visit, change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data, and block cookies in your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this page.
