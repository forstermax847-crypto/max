Insurance

Recognised by Bupa, AXA Health, Vitality, Aviva, WPA and Simplyhealth. Insured patients usually need a GP referral and an authorisation number before the first appointment.

What to bring

Any previous imaging or letters, a list of medications, your insurer's authorisation if applicable, and your questions. You are welcome to bring someone with you.

Location

Private consultations and surgery take place at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital in Wroughton, on the southern edge of Swindon, close to junction 15 of the M4 and within easy reach of Marlborough, Cirencester, Chippenham and Newbury. There is free parking at the hospital.
