Legal

Privacy and cookie policy

How your personal information is used when you contact the practice or use this website, and your rights under UK data protection law.

Miss Samantha Williams takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information is collected through this website and in the course of arranging and providing private care, why it is collected, how long it is kept and the rights you have over it. It applies to the private practice; information held about you as an NHS patient is governed by the NHS trust's own policies.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Miss Samantha Williams, Consultant Oncoplastic Breast Surgeon, practising privately at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, Moormead Road, Wroughton, Swindon SN4 9DD. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to swilliamsbreastsurgeon@gmail.com or by post to the practice at the address above, marked for the attention of the medical secretary.

Information we collect

When you contact us

If you call, email, message on WhatsApp or use the enquiry form, we collect the details you give us: typically your name, phone number, email address, the reason for your enquiry, how you would like to be contacted and any message you include. We ask you not to include detailed medical information in the enquiry form; this is better discussed at your consultation.

When you become a patient

Arranging and providing care involves collecting and creating health records: your medical history, examination findings, imaging and test results, correspondence with your GP and other clinicians, consent forms, and details needed for billing and insurance. Where your care is provided at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, the hospital also holds records about you under its own privacy policy.

When you use the website

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site (pages visited, how you arrived, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type). IP addresses are anonymised. No analytics cookies are set unless you accept them. See the cookies section below.

Why we use your information, and the legal basis

  • Responding to your enquiry and arranging appointments — necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract for care.
  • Providing clinical care and keeping accurate records — necessary for the provision of health care, and in your vital interests. Health information is "special category" data; it is processed under the specific provisions for health care in UK data protection law.
  • Communicating with your GP, the hospital, insurers and other clinicians involved in your care — necessary for the provision of care and, for insurers, to perform the contract with you.
  • Billing and accounting — necessary to perform the contract and to comply with legal obligations.
  • Website analytics — only with your consent.
  • Complying with legal and professional obligations, including General Medical Council requirements, clinical governance and responding to complaints.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Your information may be shared, only as necessary for your care or to meet legal obligations, with: BMI The Ridgeway Hospital and its clinical staff; your GP; other clinicians involved in your care such as radiologists, pathologists, anaesthetists and plastic surgeons; your private medical insurer, with your authority; the practice's medical secretary and secretarial/billing services; and regulators or courts where required by law. Service providers who process data on our behalf — such as the enquiry-form provider and email services — are bound by contract to protect it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment are deleted within 12 months. Clinical records are retained in line with the Records Management Code of Practice for Health and Social Care — generally for at least 8 years after your last treatment, and longer in some circumstances (for example, records relating to implants, or for patients under 18). Financial records are kept for 6 years as required by law.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access the information we hold about you; have inaccurate information corrected; ask for information to be erased where there is no good reason to keep it; restrict or object to certain processing; receive your data in a portable form; and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. To exercise any of these rights, contact the practice using the details above. We will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites you visit. This site uses two kinds:

Strictly necessary

A single setting is stored in your browser to remember whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, so we do not ask you on every page. This is not a tracking cookie and is not shared with anyone.

Analytics (optional)

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*, lasting up to 2 years) to distinguish visitors and measure how the site is used. This helps us understand which information patients find useful. Data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy, with IP anonymisation enabled. You can decline analytics cookies when you first visit, change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data, and block cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
