Raising a concern or complaint

If something about your care has not been right, please tell us. Every concern is taken seriously and used to improve the practice. Raising a concern will not affect the care you receive.

Step one: tell the practice

Many concerns can be resolved quickly and informally. Speak to Miss Williams at your appointment, or contact her medical secretary, Tracy Greenside, on 07354 658365 or at swilliamsbreastsurgeon@gmail.com. If you would prefer to put your concern in writing, send it to the practice at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, Moormead Road, Wroughton, Swindon SN4 9DD, marked "Complaint, private and confidential".

Please include your name and contact details, the dates and nature of the care concerned, what went wrong from your point of view, and what you would like to happen as a result. If you are complaining on behalf of someone else, their written consent is needed before information about their care can be shared.

What happens next

  • Your complaint is acknowledged within 3 working days.
  • It is investigated, with the opportunity to discuss it in person or by phone if you wish.
  • You receive a full written response within 20 working days. If the investigation needs longer, you are told why and given a revised date.
  • The response explains what was found, any action taken, and, where things went wrong, an apology and an explanation of how recurrence will be prevented.

Complaints are recorded and reviewed as part of clinical governance. Your records remain confidential, and information is shared only with those involved in investigating the complaint.

Step two: the hospital

Care provided at BMI The Ridgeway Hospital, including nursing, facilities, anaesthetic and ward care, is also covered by the hospital's own complaints process. You may raise a concern with the hospital directly, and the practice will help you do so. Where a complaint involves both the hospital and Miss Williams's clinical care, they work together to give you a single response.

To complete before publication: insert the hospital's complaints contact details (patient services or complaints manager, phone and email) from its current patient information.

Step three: independent review

If you remain dissatisfied after the practice and the hospital have responded, you can ask for an independent review. For private healthcare in England this is provided by the Independent Sector Complaints Adjudication Service (ISCAS), of which the hospital's operator is a subscriber. ISCAS can be contacted at iscas.cedr.com, by email at info@iscas.org.uk, or on 020 7536 6091. ISCAS normally expects the first two stages to have been completed and a request for adjudication to be made within six months of the final response.

Concerns about a doctor's fitness to practise

The General Medical Council (GMC) regulates all doctors in the UK. If your concern is about a doctor's professional conduct or fitness to practise, rather than the service received, you can contact the GMC at gmc-uk.org or on 0161 923 6602. Miss Williams's GMC registration number is 6130038.

Regulation

BMI The Ridgeway Hospital is registered with and inspected by the Care Quality Commission (CQC), the independent regulator of health and social care in England. The CQC does not investigate individual complaints, but you can share your experience with it at cqc.org.uk.

Clinical negligence

If you believe you have been harmed by negligent care and wish to seek compensation, you may want to take independent legal advice. Miss Williams is a member of the Medical Defence Union. A complaint through this procedure does not affect your legal rights.
