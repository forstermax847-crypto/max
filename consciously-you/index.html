How refills work

Bring a jar. Any jar.

First time in a refill shop? It takes about two minutes, and we'll happily walk you through it.

Bring a container

Any clean jar, bottle or tub works. Forgot one? Borrow or buy one in the shop.

Weigh, then fill

We weigh your empty container first — that's the tare — so you never pay for what you brought in.

Pay by weight

You only buy what you'll actually use. Less waste in your bin, and usually less on the receipt too.
